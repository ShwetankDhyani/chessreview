H2H — head-to-head form prep

ChessReview H2H is free opponent form prep for club players. Paste a Chess.com or Lichess username and see trends across their last 100 games — score, tilt, White/Black results, and how they tend to lose — without waiting on a full Stockfish review.

What H2H is for

  • Rematch prep when you face the same opponent again
  • Quick scouting from public results (not engine analysis)
  • Optional compare against your linked ChessReview profile

How it fits with game review

Use free unlimited game review after a game for move classifications, accuracy, and Stockfish lines. Use H2H before the next game for recent-form context.
