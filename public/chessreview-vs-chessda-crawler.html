ChessReview vs Chessda

Two free, no-account chess game reviewers with no subscriptions and no daily limits. ChessReview adds realistic 3D Over-The-Board (OTB) view, H2H opponent form prep, and club-player coaching insights.

Key Comparison

  • Price & Limits: Both 100% free with unlimited reviews.
  • Advertisements: Both have zero display ads.
  • 3D Board View: ChessReview features a realistic 3D board with weighted Staunton pieces; Chessda is 2D only.
  • Opponent Scouting: ChessReview includes H2H to prep against your next opponent's last 100 games; Chessda does not.
  • Engine: Both run Stockfish directly in the browser via WebAssembly for complete game privacy.
