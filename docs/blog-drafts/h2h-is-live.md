# H2H is live

Quick one: there's a new page at [/h2h](https://www.chessreview.org/h2h).

It's for those moments where you've got a game in five minutes and you want a feel for how someone's been playing lately — without waiting on a full engine review.

### What you get
Paste a Chess.com or Lichess username and we pull their last ~100 games. No Stockfish — just public results and PGN headers. From that you get:

- Score, TPR, and a simple tilt read
- Form over the sample
- How they score with White vs Black
- How they tend to lose (time, resign, mate)

You can also compare against your linked profile if you want a quick side-by-side.

### Why bother
Full game review is still the main thing. This is the lighter side dish — useful before a match, or when you're just curious about someone's recent stretch.

If something looks off, reply here. Happy to tune it.
