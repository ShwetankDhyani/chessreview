# Opponent Prep is live

Quick one: there’s a new page at [/prep](https://www.chessreview.org/prep).

It’s for those moments where you’ve got a game in five minutes and you want a feel for how someone’s been playing lately — without waiting on a full engine review.

### What you get
Paste a Chess.com or Lichess username and we pull their last ~100 games. No Stockfish grind — just the PGN headers and public results. From that we sketch out:

- Score, TPR, and a simple tilt read
- Form trend over the sample
- How they score with White vs Black
- How they tend to lose (time, resign, mate)

There’s also a compare-against-your-linked-profile option if you want a quick head-to-head snapshot.

### Why bother
Full game review is still the main thing. This is the lighter side dish — useful before a match, or when you’re just curious about someone’s recent stretch.

If something looks off or you want another angle on the dossier, reply here. Happy to tune it.
